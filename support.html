Frequently asked questions

Purchases

I bought a pack or Premium but it does not show

Open Settings > Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it. Packs are built into the app: once restored, they unlock at once, no download needed.

I changed phone. Will I keep my purchases?

Yes. Install GeoGraph on the new device, sign in with the same store account, then open Settings > Restore purchases. Your progress, however, stays on the old device: it is stored only on the device, never online.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: Google Play refund help. Steam: help.steampowered.com. itch.io: write to us through the contact form, as the seller handles refunds there.

What does Premium include?

Every country pack (France, USA, and those to come) and no ads between quizzes. You can still watch a video by choice for a reward.

Ads and privacy

Why does the app show ads?

The World map is free for everyone. Ads between some quizzes help fund the game. Premium removes them. The PC version has no ads.

How do I change my ad consent?

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, open Settings > Privacy choices in the app. On iPhone and iPad you can also allow or deny tracking under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Your progress is saved only on your device. Use Settings > Erase progress in the app, or delete the app. We hold no data about you. Ad data is managed by Google at My Ad Center.

Playing

Can I play offline?

Yes. Every quiz, map and pack works without a connection. Only ads and purchases need the internet.

How do I stop daily quiz reminders?

Turn them off in the app's settings, or in your device's notification settings.

How do I change the language?

GeoGraph follows your device language (English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese or Italian). You can also pick a language in the app's settings.

The map shows a border or name I disagree with

Maps follow common international usage and are credited in Settings > Credits. If you see a mistake, please tell us through the contact form.
