Find it on the map

A country, a state, a département: find the one asked for, or name the one lit up. The smallest islands get a marker, so Tuvalu is never out of reach. Pan and pinch as you like.

02

Flags, capitals, countries

Every kind of question, both ways round. Pick from four answers, or type it yourself. A small typo won't cost you the point.

03

Complete the map

No choices, no hints. Type every country you can think of and watch the world fill in, one name at a time.

04

Levels and stars

From the countries everyone knows to the ones nobody does. Up to three stars per level, or build a quiz of your own: any regions, any questions, any length.

05

Learn, then explore

Cards that remember what you know and bring back what you forget. Then wander the map freely: every country with its capital, flag and region.

One quiz a day

The same daily quiz for everyone, wherever you play. Keep the streak alive. Get a reminder each day and share your score.
